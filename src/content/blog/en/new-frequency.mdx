For a year this domain was a single screen: a name, three paragraphs and two
links. It did the job, but it told instead of showing. This redesign is the
jump from card to site: projects, talks, a blog and a hero that draws live
radio signals.

## The decisions

**Astro, static, on Cloudflare Pages.** The site compiles to plain HTML. No
server, no database, no attack surface beyond the files being served.

**A hand-rolled shader instead of a 3D library.** The hero background is a
WebGL2 fragment shader a few dozen lines long: a spectrum waterfall, drifting
carriers and an FFT band, the visual vocabulary of anyone who has ever used an
SDR. Three.js would have cost 150+ KB to draw a rectangle.

**Zero trackers, zero third parties.** No request leaves this domain: no
analytics, no external fonts, no CDN. Typography is whatever your system
ships. What you read here tells no one anything.

**Actually bilingual.** Spanish at the root, English under `/en/`, with the
full `hreflang` pair and a toggle that remembers your choice.

The site's code is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/jdpinedac/juanpineda.co) under the MIT license.
